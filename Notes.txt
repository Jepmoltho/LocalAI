Here's a recipe for a Mezcalita Xipe:

Ingredients:

* 2 oz Mezcal
* 1 oz Grapefruit juice
* 1/2 oz Honey syrup (1:1 honey and water, dissolved)
* 1/2 oz Lime juice
* 2 dashes of Smoked salt bitters
* Ice
* Lime wheel, for garnish
* Sprig of rosemary, for garnish

Instructions:

1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Add Mezcal, grapefruit juice, honey syrup, and lime juice.
3. Shake until chilled.
4. Strain into a rocks glass filled with ice.
5. Add 2 dashes of Smoked salt bitters.
6. Garnish with a lime wheel and a sprig of rosemary.

This drink combines the smoky flavor of Mezcal with the tartness of grapefruit and the sweetness of honey syrup. The Smoked salt bitters add a unique smoky flavor that complements the Mezcal nicely.
