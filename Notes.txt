Embeddings

Principle: an embedding model turns text into a list of numbers (a vector) that represents its meaning. 
Texts with similar meanings get vectors that sit close together, even if they share no words: “smoky and bitter” ends up near “Mezcal Negroni”. 
Embeddings are the basis of semantic search and RAG. They’re a different kind of model from chat models: they output numbers, not text.

Your coverage: this is the one topic the playground didn’t exercise. A 20-minute experiment would close the gap before Phase 4:

ollama pull nomic-embed-text
Create an IEmbeddingGenerator (OllamaSharp’s OllamaApiClient implements it) and embed 5–10 cocktail descriptions.
Embed a query like “something smoky and bitter” and rank the cocktails by cosine similarity, written as a plain loop.

You’ll see that it finds meaning rather than keywords. You’ll also see where it fails: an exact ingredient search can lose to “vibes”, which is why Phase 4 adds hybrid search.
